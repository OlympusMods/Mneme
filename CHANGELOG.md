# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Text notes and checklists with colors, a paper, plain or black material, and a button that inserts your coordinates
- Pin notes to the side of the screen and scale them
- Timers with presets and a toast and sound when they ring
- Calculator that understands stacks and shulkers, with history
- Macros with a small script language, started by a key, joining a world, dying, a timer or a chat message
- Menu and settings built on the Proteus library
- The menu is a card in the Proteus hub, next to every other mod built on Proteus
